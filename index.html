ABOUT ROBLAST

A small, ambitious Roblox studio focused on games that are easy to start and hard to leave.

We make games for the moment when “I'll play for five minutes” turns into “one more round.”

RoBlast is built around fast ideas, sharp gameplay loops and constant iteration. We care about the first five minutes, the hundredth session and everything that makes a player want to come back.
